How it works

1. Join or create a group

Choose your contribution amount and cycle (weekly/fortnightly/monthly).

2. Add members

Invite 5-20 people, set the payout order, and lock the rules.

3. Pay into the pot

Each member contributes on time; late payments apply penalties.

4. Receive the pot

One member collects the total minus platform fee (1%). Everyone evens out by cycle end.

Features

Automated collection

Track due dates, status and receipts transparently in the ledger.

Penalty engine

Late payment penalty applies automatically (default 10% per missed cycle).

Progress dashboard

Live balance by member, group total, and payout schedule.

Secure UK Data

Your data is saved in secure Postgres, fully auditable and easy to export.

Group governance rules

  • 1. Each member pays agreed contribution before due date.
  • 2. One treasurer handles collection and payout.
  • 3. Payout order is fixed at setup; changes need unanimous consent.
  • 4. Late payment = 10% penalty automatically added to next cycle.
  • 5. No early withdrawal unless full group agrees and covers shortfall.
  • 6. Disputes handled by majority vote; member removal is group decision.
  • 7. One cycle = N member payments; option to restart after completion.
  • 8. All transactions logged with full transparency.
